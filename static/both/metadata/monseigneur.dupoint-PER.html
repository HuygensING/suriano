PRIMARY NAMEMonseigneur Dupoint
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESPalatine agent in France in 1622 and 1623
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONA Frenchman who was part of the Palatinate court in The Hague around 1620. His son was a page of Elizabeth Stuart. Frederick sends him as an agent to Louis XIII in 1622 and 1623.
