DEATH YEAR1637
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONIn January 1617 a royal decree accusses him of lese majeste but after the death of Concini managed to regain favour of Louis XIII. The French crown supported his claim as successor to Duchy of Mantua and Monferrato and following a devastating conflict he became the Duke of Mantua and Montferrato from 1627 to 1637
