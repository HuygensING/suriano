PRIMARY NAMECharles I Louis and Elisabeth of the Palatinate
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESChildren of Frederick V of the Palatinate and Elizabeth Stuart
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONBoth had been born in Heidelberg: Charles in 1617 and Elisabeth in 1618. Neither of them joined their parents to Prague, but stayed in Heidelberg with their grandmother Louise Juliana of Nassau. Elizabeth stayed in Heidelberg until 1627.
