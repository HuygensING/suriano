PRIMARY NAMEJan III van Beieren, Lord of Schagen
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESSixth lord of Schagen, Borgharen and Barsingerhorn. He was admitted to the Knighthood of Holland and West Friesland, was appointed committeeman in the Council of State and member of the States General, and was appointed committeeman in the Council of Holland.
BIRTH YEAR1544
DEATH YEAR1618
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONIn 1618, he and Rutger Wessel van de Boetzelaer urged the release of Oldenbarnevelt.
