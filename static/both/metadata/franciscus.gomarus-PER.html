PRIMARY NAMEFranciscus Gomarus
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESProfessor of Theology at Leiden University 1594-1611 and Groningen University 1618-1641
BIRTH YEAR1563
DEATH YEAR1641
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONCame into conflict with Jacob Arminius, primarily concerning their differing views on predestination. His followers were the ’Gomarists’ or ’contraremonstranten’. Took a leading role at the Synod of Dordrecht.
