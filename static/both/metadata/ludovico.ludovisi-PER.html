PRIMARY NAMELudovico Ludovisi
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESNephew of Alessandro Ludovisi
BIRTH YEAR1595
DEATH YEAR1632
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONThe day after the election of his nephew as Gregory XV, he was appointed Cardinal at the age of 25. He was also appointed as governor of Fermo, legate of Avignon and Archbishop of Bologna in 1621. He played an instrumental role in papacy of Gregory XV.
