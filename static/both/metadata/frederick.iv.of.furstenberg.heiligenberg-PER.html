GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONHe passed away on 8 August 1617, Suriano refers to him in letter dated 29 August. His son Egon VIII of Fürstenberg-Heiligenberg (1588-1635) was a cleric before succeeding his father and it seems unlikely that Suriano refers to him as raising troops in 1617.
