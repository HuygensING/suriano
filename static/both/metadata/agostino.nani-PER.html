PRIMARY NAMEAgostino Nani
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESVenetian patrician who held various important diplomatic positions.
BIRTH YEAR1555
DEATH YEAR1627
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONBefore his appointment as bailo in Constantinople (1600-1604), he held various diplomatic positions, most importantly as Venetian ambassador in Spain (1594-1598). In 1604, he was appointed ambassador to Pope Clement VIII. He was elected extraordinary ambassador in 1610 on the occasion of Louis XIII’s accession to the throne. In July 1612, he was appointed extraordinary ambassador to congratulate Emperor Matthias.
