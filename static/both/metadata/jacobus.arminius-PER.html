PRIMARY NAMEJacobus Arminius
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESPriest; Professor of Theology at Leiden University 1603-1611

GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONCame into conflict with Franciscus Gomarus, primarily concerning their differing views on predestination, on which Arminius’ views were less strict and absolute. His followers were the ’Arminians’ or ’remonstranten’.
