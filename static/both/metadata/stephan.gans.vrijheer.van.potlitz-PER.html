PRIMARY NAMEStephan Gans, vrijheer van Potlitz
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESGerman commander in the army of States General
DEATH YEAR1626
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONRitmeester in army of States General. He was married to Helena van Brederode van Kloetinge. He was involved in the attack on archducal Netherlands in 1622 and commanded together with Villiers the troops towards Brussels. In October 1626, he was taken prisoner by Hendirck van den Berg and shortly after passed awy.
Archival references or contemporary referencesThe date of his death is on the basis of the Resolutions of the States General on incoming letter by Brederode (12 november 1626)
