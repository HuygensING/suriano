OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESSon of Álvaro de Bazán y Guzmán, 1st Marquess of Santa Cruz (1526-1588), a Spanish admiral and landlord who was the intended commander for the Spanish Armada. Alvaro II was governor of the Duchy of Milan from 1630 to 1631, he was also commander of the Spanish navy, army general during the Thirty Years’ War, member of the Spanish Court Martial and Council of State, Marquis of El Viso, Lord of Valdepeñas and Grand Duke of Spain.
