PRIMARY NAMEJoan Wolfert van Brederode
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESMember of the noble ’House of Brederode’. Lord of Brederode, Vianen, Noordeloos and Ameide. Officer and statesman.
BIRTH YEAR1599
DEATH YEAR1655
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONMember of important noble family from Holland. He married Anna-Johanna van Nassau-Siegen in 1619. He was first noble in the college of the Ridderschap. Made his career in the state army under Stadholder Frederick-Henry of Nassau, later Brederode was a pronounced supporter of De Witt. From 1630 to 1655 he was the governor of s’Hertogenbosch.
