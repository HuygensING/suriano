OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESDuke of Lorraine from 1608 until 1624
BIRTH YEAR1563
DEATH YEAR1624
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONDue to the wars of religion, he was not educated at the French court like his father and grandfather before him. He fought on side of Catholics during these conflicts and was a throne predent for the crown in 1593. As Duke of Lorraine, he was a defender of Counter-reformation. In the Holy Roman Empire he tried to maintain a more neutral position
