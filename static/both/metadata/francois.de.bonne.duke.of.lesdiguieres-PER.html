PRIMARY NAMEFrançois de Bonne, Duke of Lesdiguiéres
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESImportant Huguenots leader in Dauphines during the French wars of religion and became a marshal of France in 1609
BIRTH YEAR1543
DEATH YEAR1626
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONHe was in charge of Dauphine. In 1622, he converted to Catholicism publicly and became Connetable de France.
