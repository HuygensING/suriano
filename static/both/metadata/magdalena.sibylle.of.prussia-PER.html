PRIMARY NAMEMagdalena Sibylle of Prussia
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESElectress of Saxony
BIRTH YEAR1586
DEATH YEAR1659
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONShe was the youngest daughter of the Duke of Prussia and raised in Köningsberg. In 1607 she married with Duke of Saxony with whom she had ten children. She was strongly in favour of Gustav II Adolf of Sweden and did not approve her husband’s position later in the Thirty Years’ War to conclude a peace deal with the Emperor.
