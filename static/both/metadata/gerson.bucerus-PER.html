PRIMARY NAMEGerson Bucerus
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESPreacher of Flemish descent, born in England. From 1588, he was pastor in Veere. He officiated at the blessing of the marriage of Hugo de Groot and Maria van Reigersberch in 1608. His publication called ’Disseratio de gubernatione ecclesiae’ aroused the wrath of the English king James I. He also contributed to the Dutch translation of the Bible.
