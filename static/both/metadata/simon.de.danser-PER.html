PRIMARY NAMESimon de Danser
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESDutch corsair
BIRTH YEAR1579
DEATH YEAR1611
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONDutch corsair, orginally from Dordrecht. He was active in service of Pasha of Algiers, subsequently pardoned for his actions by Henry IV in 1609. Numerous accounts claim he was killed 1610/1611.
