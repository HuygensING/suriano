PRIMARY NAMEJohann Jacob Bronckhorst Batenburg
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESMilitary commander in imperial service
BIRTH YEAR1582
DEATH YEAR1630
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONTogether with his father he served in the Spanish army (1603), after conclusion of Twelve Years’ Truce he started serving archduke Leopold and his regiment fought from 1618 onwards. In 1620 he fought under Maximilian of Bavaria at the Battle of White Mountain. In 1622 he was Field Marshall of the joint imperial-bavarian army and played a crucial role to keep Mansfeld and Christian the Younger out of Westphalia
