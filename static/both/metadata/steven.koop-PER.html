PRIMARY NAMESteven Koop
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESCaptain who served in the regiment of Ernst Casimir of Nassau-Dietz. Colonel of infantry with John Ernst of Nassau in Venice. He killed the French captain ’De Goleme’ in a sword fight. He was not send back to his regiment. The Venetian state pardoned him in January 1618
