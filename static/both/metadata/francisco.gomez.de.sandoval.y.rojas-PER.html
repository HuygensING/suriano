PRIMARY NAMEFrancisco Gómez de Sandoval y Rojas
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESA Spanish statesman. He served Philip II as a courtier and made himself ’favourite’ to the young Philip III.
BIRTH YEAR1553
DEATH YEAR1625
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONHe fell out of favour and was forced to leave the court on 4 October 1618. As a protection and an attempt to retain power, he was made cardinal by Pope Paul V.
