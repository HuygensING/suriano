PRIMARY NAMEJohann Georg Melander, Count of Holzappel
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESBrother of Peter Melander
BIRTH YEAR1579
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONBorn as son of a German farmer (Peter Eppelmann in Nederhadamar), he was raised together with his two other brothers by his uncle in Northern Netherlands, who was a secretary of Maurice of Nassau. In contrast to his older and younger brother, it seems that Johan Georg Holtzappel did not join the military. We know very little about his life. On basis of Suriano’s correspondence, we know he negotiated on behalf of his brother Peter Melander.
