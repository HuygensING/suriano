PRIMARY NAMEPompeo Giustiniani
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESGenoese nobleman in the service of Venice during the war of Gradisca
BIRTH YEAR1569
DEATH YEAR1616
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONA veteran of war in the Low Countries. He fought in the Habsburg army as a military commander from 1587 until 1597 and returned in 1601. He fought under Spinola during the siege of Ostend where he lost his arm and became known as ’il braccio ferro’. In 1613 he decided to leave the Habsburg Netherlands and serve the Venetian Republic.
