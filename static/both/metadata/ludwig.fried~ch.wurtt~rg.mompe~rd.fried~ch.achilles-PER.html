PRIMARY NAMELudwig Friedrich von Württemberg-Mömpelgard; Friedrich Achilles von Württemberg and Magnus von Württemberg
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESBrothers of Johann Friedrich of Würtemburg
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONIn 1617 the brothers settled the inheritance issue. Ludwig Friederich (1586-1631) received Mömpelgard, Friederich Achilles (1591-1631) received Schloss Neuenstadt and Magnus von Württemberg (1594-1622) obtained Schloss Neuenbürg. Magnus died in May 1622 during battle of Wimpfen
