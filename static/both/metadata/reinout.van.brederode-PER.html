PRIMARY NAMEReinout van Brederode
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESJurist and diplomat, member of prominent noble family in Holland
BIRTH YEAR1567
DEATH YEAR1633
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONHe was lord of Veenhuizen, Spanbroek, Oosthuizen, Etersheim, Hobrede and Kwadijk. His father Lancelot played a crucial role during the Siege of Haarlem. Reinoud was a son-in-law of Oldenbarnevelt and was appointed as a counselor of Hoge Raad of Holland and Zeeland. In 1616 he was appointed ambassador to Russia. He shared in the misfortunes of the fate of his father-in-law and his family, he was no longer seen as a member of Dutch nobility.
