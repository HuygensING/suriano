PRIMARY NAMEWilhelm Kettler, Duke of Courland
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESDuke of Courland until 1617
BIRTH YEAR1574
DEATH YEAR1640
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONHe and his brother Friedrich (1569-1642) divided the duchy. Wilhelm ruled the Courland part and Friedrich Semigallia. Willem was stripped of his title and banished from the duchy by the Regional Assembly of Courland in 1617. In his letter, Suriano refers to a ’duca di Curlant’ who was expelled and dispossessed, so we have identified him as Wilhelm, and not Friedrich.
