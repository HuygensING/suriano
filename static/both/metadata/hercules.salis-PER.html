PRIMARY NAMEHercules Salis
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESNobleman from the Three Leagues
BIRTH YEAR1566
DEATH YEAR1620
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONMember of the Salis (Salice) family branch Salis-Soglio-Grüsch (Cruscio). He had studied in Heidelberg and Tübingen. He was sent as a diplomatic agent to Venice in 1604 and met with the doge. He maintained close contact with Venetian agent Padavino and played a crucial political role in seeking protection from Venice during the growing tensions with Habsburg over the Valtellina. He was a promotor of reformed Protestantism. He passed away in Venice during diplomatic mission on 28 september 1620.
