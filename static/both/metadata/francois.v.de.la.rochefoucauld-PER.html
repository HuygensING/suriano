PRIMARY NAMEFrançois V de la Rochefoucauld
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESFrench nobleman
BIRTH YEAR1588
DEATH YEAR1650
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONAs the eldest son, he inherited the title as Comte de la Rochefoucauld in 1591. He abjured his reformed beliefs and became a Catholic. He receives several military appointments by Louis XIII, including lieutenant general of Guyenne in 1615-1616 and plays a role in defeating the Huguenots in Poitou. In 1622 Louis XIII raises his territories to level of a Duchy.
