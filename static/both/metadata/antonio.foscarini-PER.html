PRIMARY NAMEAntonio Foscarini
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESVenetian nobleman and ambassador
BIRTH YEAR APPROX1570
DEATH YEAR1622
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONVenetian ambassador to France (1607-1610) and later to England (1611-1615). In 1615 he was accussed of selling state secrets to Spain, and arrested upon arrival in Venice but only realsed three years later without charge. In 1620, Foscarini was elected to the senate, but was arrested in 1622 accused of disclosing state secrets. He was sentenced to death by council of Ten for high treason and a year later posthumously exonerated.
