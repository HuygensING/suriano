PRIMARY NAMESimon Digby
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESStuart resident ambassador in Vienna from May 1621 to December 1622
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONLittle is known about the life of Simon Digby. In Vienna he shared responsibilities with his cousin Lord John Digby to negotiate a solution for the Upper Palatinate with the Emperor. He stayed in Vienna after John Digby left in 1621.
