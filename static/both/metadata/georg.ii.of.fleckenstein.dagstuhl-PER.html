PRIMARY NAMEGeorg II of Fleckenstein Dagstuhl
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESthe last baron of the house of Fleckenstein
BIRTH YEAR1588
DEATH YEAR1644
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONHe pursued a military career and was a colonel in army of the Protestant Union. In 1621, after the union was dissolved, he entered into the service of Friederich V of Baden-Durlach. A year later he retired to his barony and tried to remain neutral but eventually went into exile in Strasbourg.
