PRIMARY NAMEDudley Diggs and Maurice Abbot
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESTwo English envoys sent to Dutch Republic to negotiate with VOC
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONSuriano wrongly identifies one of these individuals as the brother of John King. Abbot was an English merchant and one of director of East India Company. Dudley Digges was a member of parliament and investor in Virginia Company. The Dutch had infringed on the 1619 agreement to divide the spice trade between the two companies. The two men were sent to negotiate a better treaty.
