PRIMARY NAMEAngelo Badoer
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESVenetian patrician and diplomat
BIRTH YEAR1565
DEATH YEAR1630
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONIn favour of papacy and Philip III, he was convicted to one year imprisonement in 1607 and in 1612 he is again accussed of divulging Venetian state secrets to Spain and banished from Venice for life. He lived in exile for 18 years and moved around Europe. In 1620 he travelled in the Netherlands using a pseudonym.
