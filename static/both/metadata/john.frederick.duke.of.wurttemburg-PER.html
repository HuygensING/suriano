PRIMARY NAMEJohn Frederick, Duke of Württemburg
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESDuke of Württemberg from 1608-1628
BIRTH YEAR1582
DEATH YEAR1628
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONHe joined Protestant Union in 1608 and thus also supported Frederick V’s cause. In 1621 he led unsuccesful entry into battle with army of union in 1621. After the battle of Wimpfen in 1622, despite neutrality agreement, his territory was raided by imperial trooops.
