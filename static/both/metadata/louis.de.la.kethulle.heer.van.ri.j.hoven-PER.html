PRIMARY NAMELouis de la Kethulle, heer van Ri(j)hoven
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESGovernor of Bergen op Zoom
BIRTH YEAR1565
DEATH YEAR1631
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONOriginally from Ghent, in 1584 following the city’s surrender to Alexander Farnese, he fled together with his father, brother and sisters to the Dutch Republic. Louis entered in the service of States’ army and was appointed in 1618 as the governor of Bergen-Op-Zoom. In 1622 he managed maintain the city into Dutch possession following siege led by Spinola.
