PRIMARY NAMEChristoffel Biesman
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESMember of Nijmegen Magistrate from 1600-1618, first time mayor in 1606. Member for Gelre in the States General (1606-1618)
BIRTH YEAR APPROX1570
DEATH YEAR1634
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONA very good friend of Johan van Oldenbarnevelt. In 1618 he had to leave his functions in magistrate of Nijmegen and also the States General.
