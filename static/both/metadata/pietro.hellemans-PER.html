PRIMARY NAMEPietro Hellemans
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESMerchant
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONSon of Carlo Hellemans, who had succesfully established an international trading company together with his brothers and nephews, based in Venice with branches in Constantinople and Goa. In the early seventeenth century the family company and its large commercial network collapsed. Pietro Hellemans left Venice and returned to Antwerp.
