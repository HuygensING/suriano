PRIMARY NAMEPeter Ernst, Count of Mansfeld
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESA Catholic commander who served Protestant princes as a mercenary from 1610
BIRTH YEAR UNCERTAIN1580
DEATH YEAR1626
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONIllegitimate son of Peter I of Mansfeld, who had served the Spanish-Habsburg monarchy. From 1610 onwards he started to fight on behalf of the Protestant Princes. He played a crucial role during the first phases of the Thirty Years’ War.
