PRIMARY NAMEMaurice of Nassau
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESStadtholder of Holland and Zeeland (1585), Utrecht, Gelre and Overijssel (1590) and Groningen (1620), Prince of Orange (1618)
BIRTH YEAR1567
DEATH YEAR1625
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONHe succeeded his father, William of Orange as stadtholder and as captain of army of States General obtained many successes against the Spanish-Habsburg army. During the Truce, he entered into a conflict with Johan van Oldenbarnevelt. He never married but had several illegitimate childeren
