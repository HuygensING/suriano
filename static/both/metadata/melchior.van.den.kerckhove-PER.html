PRIMARY NAMEMelchior van den Kerckhove
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESAdmiral in Venetian service; buccaneer
DEATH YEAR1619
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONBuccaneer enlisted as an explorer by the Brabantsche Compagnie and Henry IV of France to America, but failed to reach it. Enlisted as an admiral in Venetian service in 1618.
