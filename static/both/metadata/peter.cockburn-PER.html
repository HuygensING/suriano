PRIMARY NAMEPeter Cockburn
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESScottish captain
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONThe identification of this captain as Peter remains uncertain. A captain Cockburn appears in regiment of sir Andrew Gray, however, without a first name so it is unclear whether he is the same individual who later fought in Danish service.
