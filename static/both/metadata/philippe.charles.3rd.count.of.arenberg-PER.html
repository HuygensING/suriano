PRIMARY NAMEPhilippe-Charles, 3rd Count of Arenberg
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESCount of Arenberg; Duke of Aarschot since 1616
BIRTH YEAR1587
DEATH YEAR1640
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONSon of Charles of Arenberg and Anne de Croÿ. He was an influential figure in the political life of the Habsburg Netherlands. He was a member of the Order of the Golden Fleece. Fell from grace in 1634 and was placed under house arrest in Madrid until his death.
