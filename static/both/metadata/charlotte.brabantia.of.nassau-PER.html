PRIMARY NAMECharlotte Brabantia of Nassau
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESDaughter of William of Orange and Charlotte de Bourbon. She had married Claude de la Trémoille, Duke of Thouars
BIRTH YEAR1580
DEATH YEAR1631
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONCharlotte Brabantia maintained close relations with Henry IV, his successor Louis XIII and Queen Mother Maria de Medici. Louis XIII gave her the leadership of the 22nd National Synod of the Reformed churches in 1617 in recognition of her mediating role in bringing about the Treaty of Loudun (1616). In 1618, she joined Boissise on diplomatic mission to The Hague to visit her brothers, but she was also an important political mediator between the French court and the Dutch Republic.
