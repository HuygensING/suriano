PRIMARY NAMEMaria Anna of Spain
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESSecond surviving daugther of Philips III
BIRTH YEAR1606
DEATH YEAR1646
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONMaria Anna played an important role in matrimonial politics of Philip III. In 1622 negotiations started for the Spanish match. In 1626, she was betrothed to Archduke Ferdinand, whom she married in 1631 later became Holy Roman Empress.
