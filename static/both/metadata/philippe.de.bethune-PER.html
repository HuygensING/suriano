PRIMARY NAMEPhilippe de Béthune
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESFrench diplomat and nobleman
BIRTH YEAR1565
DEATH YEAR1649
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONYounger brother of Henry IV’s famous minister, Maximilien de Béthune. He was appointed ambassador in the Holy Roman Empire together with Charles de Valois and Charles de L’Aubespine to negotiate with Princes of Union and Ferdinand II.
