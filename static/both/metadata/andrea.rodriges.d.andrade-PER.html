PRIMARY NAMEAndrea Rodriges d’Andrade
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESPortugese merchant, member of an international trading family
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONMerchant active in Venice, who ran a company with his brother. In 1622 the Admiralty of Amsterdam had confiscated their silk.
