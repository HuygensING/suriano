PRIMARY NAMECamillo Trevisan
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESCommander of the cavalry in the war of Gradisca
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONMember of rich Venetian family, who together with his brothers joined the Venetian army in war of Gradisca. He commanded the Albanian and Croatian cavalry. His brothers are Marco and Antonio (died in the war)
