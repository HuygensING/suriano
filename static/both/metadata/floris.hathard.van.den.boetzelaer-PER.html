PRIMARY NAMEFloris Hathard van den Boetzelaer
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESNobleman, Lord of Asperen, Rheydt, Langerack and Odenkircken
BIRTH YEAR1560
DEATH YEAR1636
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONSon of Floris van den Boetzelaer who had signed the compromise of nobles and fled in 1568 from the Low Countries. Little is known about his adult life. He owned the castle Rheydt, located in the strategic border area between the Low Countries and Jülich-Cleves. He had allowed 150 soldiers from army of States General under command of Tytfort to be garrisoned in his castle. In 1621, he was taken prisoner by Van den Bergh to press the commander of Dutch garrison to surrender.
