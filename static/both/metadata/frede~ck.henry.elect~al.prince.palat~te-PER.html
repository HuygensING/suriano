PRIMARY NAMEFrederick Henry, Electoral Prince of Palatinate
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESEldest son of Frederick V of the Palatinate and Elizabeth Stuart.
BIRTH YEAR1614
DEATH YEAR1629
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONHe accompanied his parents to Prague in 1619 and elected as successor to the crown in December 1619. In September 1620 he was sent to his custodian in Leeuwarden, Ernst Casimir, stadtholder of Frisia. He joined his parents in exile in The Hague. He drowned in 1629 and was buried in The Hague.
