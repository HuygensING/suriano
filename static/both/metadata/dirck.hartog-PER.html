PRIMARY NAMEDirck Hartog
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESCaptain of the ship Il Leon Felice (Lucky Lion)
BIRTH YEAR1580
DEATH YEAR1621
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONDutch sailor and explorer, famous for his expeditions to Australia in 1616 for VOC. In service of Trip and Nicquet he sailed with his own ship to the Adriatic in 1619.
