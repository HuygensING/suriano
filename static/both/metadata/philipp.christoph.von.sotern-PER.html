PRIMARY NAMEPhilipp Christoph von Sötern
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESPrince-Bishop of Speyer (1610-1652), became in 1623 Archbishop-Elector of Trier in 1623
BIRTH YEAR1567
DEATH YEAR1652
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONHe converted to Catholicism as a child and was educated by the Jesuits. He was a supporter of the Catholic League but increasingly opposed the Habsburgs and supported French intervention in the Thirty Years’ war. He was confirmed as Archbishop of Trier in 1623.
