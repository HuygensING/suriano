PRIMARY NAMEBartholt van Sterckenburch
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESCaptain of the guard of Maurice of Nassau
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONHe was captain of the guard of prince Maurice until 1617/1618. Later, he becomes commander of the city of Meurs. In 1620 he is briefly an agent for Joachim Ernst, margrave of Brandenburg-Ansbach, but this has something to do with his role in mustering troops for the Union..
