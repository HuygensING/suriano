PRIMARY NAMEHippolyte de Béthune
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESNobleman and politican
BIRTH YEAR1603
DEATH YEAR1665
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONSon of Philippe de Béthune (1565-1649), French ambassador in Rome. He was born in Rome, his godfather was Clemens VIII. He had fought at siege of Montauban (1621), Royan (1622) and in July 1622 he travelled to the Dutch Republic to fight at the siege of Bergen op Zoom.
