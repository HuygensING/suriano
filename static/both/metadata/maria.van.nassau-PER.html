PRIMARY NAMEMaria van Nassau
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESCountess of Buren. Maria was the second daugther of William or Orange and Anna of Egmond. She had been in charge of family possession when Philips William had been taken hostage to Spain. After the death of her father in 1584, inheritance claims clouded her relationship with Maurice of Nassau. Eventually, she was only allowed to keep Buren and in 1595 she married Philips count of Hohenlohe. From 1606 she was a widow and lived in Buren.
