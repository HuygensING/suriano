PRIMARY NAMEWandernoot

GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONAn officer among the troops of Johan Ernest of Nassau. Suriano calls him the son of the governor of Sluis, and says that Wandernoot is young but wants to emulate his father, who was a captain in past wars, but according to NNWB Charles van der Noot ’left no sons’.
