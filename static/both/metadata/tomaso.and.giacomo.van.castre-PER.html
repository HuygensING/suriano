PRIMARY NAMETomaso and Giacomo van Castre
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESNetherlandish merchants in Venice
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONOriginally from Mechelen, the brothers had settled in Venice. They were involved with financing the transport of the troops of the Count of Leeuwenstein to Venice. Lived in Venice with their other brother Amblardo, who is not mentioned by Suriano.
Bibliography: secondary literature and websitesMaartje van Gelder, Trading Places: The Netherlandish Merchants in Early Modern Venice (Brill, 2009)
