PRIMARY NAMENicholas Lanier
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESMember of entourage of Mansfeld who travelled to France in 1623
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONHighly uncertain whether this individual is Nicholas Lanier (1588-1666), court musician in England, a descendant of the French Huguenot Lanière family who were royal musicians. He was taught by his father and followed in the footsteps of his forebearers by becoming court musician in England. In 1626 he became the Master of the King’s Music. In the letters to Suriano by Rota, Lanier appears to be a courier carrying letters from military camp of Mansfeld to The Hague and was also send by Mansfeld to France in 1623.
