GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONMember of prominent noble family and fought in service of Habsburg monarchy. In 1603 he went to the Low Countries. In 1605 he played an important role in preventing a Dutch attack on Antwerp and from 1606 onwards he was governor of citadel in Antwerp until 1622.
