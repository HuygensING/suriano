PRIMARY NAMECharles de Valois
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESFrench colonel and ambassador
BIRTH YEAR1573
DEATH YEAR1650
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONIllegitimate son of Charles IX of France and Marie Touchet. He was involved in several conspiracies. He was imprisoned in Bastille from 1605 to 1616. In 1616 he was restored to his rank of colonel general of horse and fought against the rebellious nobles. In 1620 he received the duchy of Angoulême and was sent as part of important embassy to the Holy Roman Empire to negotiate with Princes of Union and Ferdinand II.
