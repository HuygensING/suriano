PRIMARY NAMEReinhard Tytfort
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESCommander of 150 soldiers
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONOriginally he was from Reval (current day Tallinn), with 150 soldiers he was garrisoned in castle Rheydt. In 1621 Van den Bergh had taken Floris van den Boetzelaer prisoner to force him to surrender. For his surrender, Tytfort was sentenced to capital punishment by the States’ army.
