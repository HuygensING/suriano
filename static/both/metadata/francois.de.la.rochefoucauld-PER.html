PRIMARY NAMEFrançois de la Rochefoucauld
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESCardinal 1607-1645; Grand Almoner of France 1618-1632; President of the Royal Council 1622-1624
BIRTH YEAR1558
DEATH YEAR1645
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONAppointed abbot of Saint-Geneviève by Louis XIII in 1619 to mitigate effects of previous canons, who had been lax in the performance of their duties. Wished to resign the cardinalate in 1635, but Pope Urban VIII denied his request.
