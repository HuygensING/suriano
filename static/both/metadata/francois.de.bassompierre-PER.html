PRIMARY NAMEFrançois de Bassompierre
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESFrench nobleman and diplomat
BIRTH YEAR1579
DEATH YEAR1646
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONAfter supporting Maria de Medici against the nobles, he subsequently remained loyal to Louis XIII. In 1621, he was appointed extraordinary diplomat to Spain. As a reward for his service against the Huguenots, he was appointed marshal of France in 1622.
