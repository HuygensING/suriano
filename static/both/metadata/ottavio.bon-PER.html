PRIMARY NAMEOttavio Bon
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESExtraordinary Venetian ambassador to France in 1616 to obtain free passage through Grison for troops.
BIRTH YEAR1552
DEATH YEAR1623
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONSuriano had been his secretary when he was proveditore di bene communali in 1603, a year later Bon became bailo in Constantinople until 1607. He was a pacifist and did not belong to anti-Roman and anti-Spanish factions within Venetian patriciate. In September 1617, he was called back from his extraordinary embassy in France and replaced by Simone Contarini who was far more anti-papal and anti-Spanish.
