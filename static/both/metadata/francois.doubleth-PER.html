DEATH YEAR UNCERTAIN< 1627
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONOriginally from a family from Mechelen, his father Philip Doubleth (d. 1573) fled in 1572. He left Mechelen in 1588 and follewed a career in footsteps of his uncle, Philip Doubleth, who was ’’Ontvanger-generaal" and president of Court of Auditors of Holland’. As paymaster, François seems to have supervised the embarkment of soldiers of Levenstein in Delfzijl and granted sums of money. He took care of prisoners in Loevenstein in 1619.
