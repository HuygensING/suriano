PRIMARY NAMEGómez Súarez de Figueroa, 3rd Duke of Feria
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESGómez had been active as a diplomat in Rome in 1607 and in France in 1610 before he was appointed viceroy of Valencia from 1615 to 1618. He was governor of the Duchy of Milan in the periods 1618-1625, viceroy of Catalonia from 1629 to 1630 and again governor of Milan 1631-1633.
BIRTH YEAR1587
DEATH YEAR1634
