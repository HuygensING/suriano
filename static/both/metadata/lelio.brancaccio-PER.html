PRIMARY NAMELelio Brancaccio
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESNeapolitan military commander, served from 1602 in the Habsburg army in the Low Countries; order of the knight of Malta
BIRTH YEAR APPROX1560
DEATH YEAR1637
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONHe was author of military manual, I carichi military, first published in Antwerp in 1610. During the Truce, he was in Naples and returned to the Low Countries in 1621 when war resumed.
