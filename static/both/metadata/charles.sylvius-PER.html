PRIMARY NAMECharles Sylvius
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESPrinter; publisher
EDITORS’ NOTES AND QUERIESThere is a Charles Silvius who is the son of the printer Willem Silvius. SG probably refers to this Charles in a footnote in the introduction to SG 1578-1579: “geswooren drucker der Staten slants Hollandt…” Unclear if this is the same Silvius as mentioned by Suriano, as active years for Willems’ son are generally listed 1580-1582.
