PRIMARY NAMEAbraham and Jan Blanchard (or Blansaert)
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESTwo brothers involved in plot to assisinate Maurice of Nassau
DEATH YEAR1623
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONBoth brothers were born in Leiden. They worked as weavers, and Jan seems to have been a Remonstrant preacher. They were involved in plot to assisinate Maurice of Nassau in 1623. They tried to flee persecution and travelled to Emden where they were arrested. They arrived in The Hague at the end of February and stayed in prison until May when they were executed.
