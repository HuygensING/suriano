PRIMARY NAMEAlbert Joachimi
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESPensionary of Goes; representative for Zeeland in States General since 1600; diplomat

GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONHe was part of several extraordinary embassies (London 1610, Sweden and Moscou 1615-1616, Holy Roman Empire 1621, England 1624) before he was appointed as the Dutch ambassador to James I/VI replacing Noël de Caron.
