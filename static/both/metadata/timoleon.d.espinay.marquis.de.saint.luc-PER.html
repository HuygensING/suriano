PRIMARY NAMETimoléon d’Espinay, marquis de Saint Luc
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESFrench nobleman and viceadmiral
BIRTH YEAR1580
DEATH YEAR1644
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONIn 1596, he joined the French army and also fought at the siege of Amiens. In 1603 he joined Sully on his embassy to London. In 1617 he is appointed as field marshal and in 1619 he named as a chevalier des orders du Roi. In 1622 he is appointed vice-admiral of France and becomes involved in siege of La Rochelle.
