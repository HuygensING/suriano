PRIMARY NAMEJan de Wael
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESMerchant from the Southern Netherlands active in Amsterdam
BIRTH YEAR1555
DEATH YEAR APPROX.1634
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONA merchant from Antwerp who worked for the Della Faille family. He had stayed in Venice between 1578 and 1592, first as a factor and later as as a partner of his uncle Jean della Faille. Subsequently he moved to Amsterdam to continue his work for Della Faille family. In 1602 he invested in VOC.
