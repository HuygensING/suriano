PRIMARY NAMENicolas de Neufville, seigneur de Villeroy
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESSecretary of State for the King of France, 1567-1588 and 1594-1617
BIRTH YEAR1543
DEATH YEAR1617
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONSecretary of State under Charles IX, Henry III, Henry IV and Louis XIII. Was sacked by Henry VIII in 1588 (together with all the king’s ministers), but reinstated by Henry IV in 1594 . Remained in office until his death in November 1617.
