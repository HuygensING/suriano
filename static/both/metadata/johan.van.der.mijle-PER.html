GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONIn the autumn of 1622, Maria van Oldenbarnevelt requested States General to allow her husband, banished in Beverwijk, to visit their son Johan on his deathbed. Apparently Johan lived for another ten year, so Suriano’s claim that he passed away is not correct.
