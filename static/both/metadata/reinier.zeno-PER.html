PRIMARY NAMEReinier Zeno
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESIn 1609 he was Podesta and Capitano of Crema. In 1614 while Savio di Terraferma, he was sent as ambassador extraordinary to the Duke of Savoy. In 1618 he was resident Ambassador to the Duke of Savoy and in 1623 he was ambassador to Urban VIII.
