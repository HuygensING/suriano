PRIMARY NAMESir Gregore Calvert
OCCUPATIONS, ROLES, and/or TITLESSecretary of State from (1619-1625) under James I/VI
BIRTH YEAR APPROX1579/1580
DEATH YEAR1632
GENERAL NOTES ON PERSONCalvert had studied at Oxford (Trinity College) and in 1606 became prviate secretary to Sir Robert Cecil. He quickly rose through ranks of public service becoming a clerk to privy council and undertaking several diplomatic mission on behalf of James I/VI, such as one to the Palatinate in 1615. He was knighted in 1617 and in 1619 he became member of Privy Council. During his tenure as secretary of state, he had to advocate the king’s support for the Spanish match. In 1625, he resigned from his post and coverted to Roman Catholicism. He was involved in colonising of Newfoundland.
